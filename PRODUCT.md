# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Delivery company owners and operators — small to mid-size courier, last-mile, and freight businesses. They're running lean, often non-technical, and tired of quoting over the phone. They use the dashboard daily to manage incoming quote requests, track jobs, and configure their widget. Customers (end users of the embedded widget) are businesses or individuals booking a delivery and expecting a clean, instant experience.

## Product Purpose

Qalt is a white-label delivery quoting and booking SaaS. Delivery companies embed a branded widget on their website so customers can get an instant price, book, and pay without phone calls or back-and-forth. The dashboard lets operators configure pricing, manage quote requests, track jobs, run analytics, and control widget appearance. Success looks like: a delivery company that never has to quote manually again.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, direct, no-nonsense. Fast like the industry it serves. Confident without being loud.

Three words: **fast, sharp, operator-grade**

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with floating cards and purple-to-pink gradients
- Overly playful or consumer-facing UI (Mailchimp vibes, rounded pastel everything)
- Enterprise bloat — tabbed dashboards with 40 nav items, heavy modal flows
- "Uber for X" startup aesthetics — neon on black, speed lines, stock delivery truck photos

## Design Principles

1. **Operators, not users** — The people using this are running a business. Treat them as professionals. Dense-but-clear over spacious-but-shallow.
2. **Speed is the product** — Every interaction should feel instant. Loading states are failures, not features.
3. **Red means action, not danger** — Red (#dc2626) is the brand accent. It signals primary actions, highlights, and energy — not errors.
4. **Earn every pixel** — No decorative elements that don't carry meaning. If it's there, it works.
5. **Dark is the native state** — The dashboard lives in the Antigravity dark theme. Design dark-first; light is a fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Standard best practices — sufficient contrast, keyboard navigability, no motion-only affordances.
